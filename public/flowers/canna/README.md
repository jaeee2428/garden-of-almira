# Canna — *Canna indica*

Indian shot, canna lily · also: balunsaying, kolintasan · Cannaceae

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | none |
| Bayong order | 10 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#d42a1a` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Clump of upright stems; large broad paddle-shaped leaves; spikes of soft, irregular flag-like red, orange or yellow flowers; round seeds hard as shot.

**Card text:** Called balunsaying or kolintasan in Bisaya (tikas in Tagalog). In Cebu, young leaves soaked in cold water are drunk to cool the body, and pounded leaves wrapped in cloth are rubbed on the skin for pasma, a folk illness blamed on sudden cold after heat. The leaf decoction is also drunk for cough.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
