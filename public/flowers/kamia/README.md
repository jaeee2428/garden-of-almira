# Kamia — *Hedychium coronarium*

white ginger lily, butterfly ginger · also: katkatan, kamya · Zingiberaceae (ginger family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | sweet, strongest in the evening |
| Bayong order | 12 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#ffffff` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Reed-like leafy stems to ~1.5 m; long lance leaves in two rows; a green cone at the top from which white butterfly-shaped flowers open a few at a time.

**Card text:** The white ginger lily, with butterfly-shaped white flowers that smell strongest in the evening; also called katkatan in Bisaya. Folk uses: a decoction of the stem as a gargle for tonsillitis, the rhizome for worms and fever, and the flowers for wreaths and bridal bouquets.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
