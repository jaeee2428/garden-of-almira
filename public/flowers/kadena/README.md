# Kadena de Amor — *Antigonon leptopus*

coral vine, chain of love · also: kadena de amor · Polygonaceae (buckwheat family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | faint |
| Bayong order | 7 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#ff7fa8` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Climbing vine with tendrils; heart-shaped leaves; long trailing sprays of small pink heart-shaped flowers.

**Card text:** The “chain of love”, a vine with heart-shaped leaves and sprays of small pink flowers. Philippine folk uses recorded for it: leaf tea for blood sugar, blossoms for high blood pressure, and leaves applied to reduce swelling and to close wounds.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
