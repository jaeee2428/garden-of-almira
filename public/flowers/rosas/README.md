# Rosas — *Rosa × hybrida*

rose · Rosaceae (rose family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | sweet (varies) |
| Bayong order | 9 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#c4112f` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Thorny shrub; leaves of 5 toothed leaflets; layered spiral of velvety petals around a tight centre.

**Card text:** A favourite of home gardens, and the flower of the Flores de Mayo and the Santacruzan processions in May, when girls bring roses and other flowers to the Virgin Mary.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
