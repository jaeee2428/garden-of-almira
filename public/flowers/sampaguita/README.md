# Sampaguita — *Jasminum sambac*

Arabian jasmine · also: manul · Oleaceae (olive family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | sweet, strongest at dusk and night |
| Bayong order | 1 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#f5f2e6` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Shrub or loose climber ~1-1.5 m; glossy oval opposite leaves; small waxy white flowers, single or double, in little clusters with pointed buds.

**Card text:** The national flower; its name comes from sumpa kita, “I promise you”. Called manul in Bisaya. Garlands go on holy images and altars. In folk medicine a decoction of the flowers is taken for fever and cough and used as an eyewash for red, swollen eyes, and bruised leaves are laid on the breasts to dry up milk.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
