# Pukingan — *Clitoria ternatea*

butterfly pea, blue ternate · also: kolokanting, balog-balog · Fabaceae (pea family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | none |
| Bayong order | 16 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#2433b0` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Twining vine; leaves of 5-7 oval leaflets; single flowers with one large deep-blue standard petal around a pale yellow-white centre; flat pods.

**Card text:** Also kolokanting, and balog-balog in Bisaya: a twining vine with one big deep-blue petal around a pale centre, grown on fences and stakes. Its young shoots, flowers and tender pods are eaten as a vegetable, and across Southeast Asia the flowers colour rice and tea blue. Folk uses: leaf poultices on swollen joints, a leaf infusion on skin eruptions, warm leaf juice with salt around an aching ear. The roots and seeds are toxic.

Sources: stuartxchange.com/Pukingan
