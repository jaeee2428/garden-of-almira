# Rosal — *Gardenia jasminoides*

gardenia, cape jasmine · Rubiaceae (coffee family)

| | |
|---|---|

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Shrub 1-1.5 m; glossy dark opposite leaves; heavily scented double white flowers in a spiral of waxy petals, ageing creamy yellow.

**Card text:** The gardenia: glossy dark leaves and heavily scented double white flowers that turn creamy yellow as they age. In the Philippines it goes into wreaths and bouquets, and its ripe yellow fruit gives a dye for cloth and food. Folk uses listed in Philippine herbal references (many from Chinese practice): a flower decoction as an eyewash for sore eyes, a leaf poultice on swollen breasts, the pounded fruit on sprains and boils.

Sources: stuartxchange.org/Rosal.html
