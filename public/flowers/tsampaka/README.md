# Tsampaka — *Magnolia champaca*

champak, joy perfume tree · also: champaca · Magnoliaceae (magnolia family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | very strong and sweet |
| Bayong order | 17 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#f2a83a` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Small tree; long glossy pointed leaves; single flowers in the leaf axils with many narrow tepals, pale yellow turning orange-yellow; slender pointed buds.

**Card text:** The champak or joy perfume tree. Its single flowers open pale yellow and turn orange-yellow, with one of the strongest scents of any garden. The flowers are strung into fragrant garlands, strewn on bridal beds and distilled into perfume and hair oil. Folk uses recorded across Asia include a bark decoction for fever and crushed leaves in oil rubbed on rheumatic joints; I found no use documented specifically in the Visayas.

Sources: stuartxchange.com/TsampakangPula
