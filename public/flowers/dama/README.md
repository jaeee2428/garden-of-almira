# Dama de Noche — *Cestrum nocturnum*

night-blooming jessamine, lady of the night · also: dama de noche · Solanaceae (nightshade family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | very strong, only at night |
| Bayong order | 5 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#e4efc2` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Loose shrub with arching stems; narrow pointed leaves; clusters of slender greenish-cream tubular flowers with 5 tiny star lobes that open after dusk.

**Card text:** “Lady of the night”: the greenish-yellow tubes open after dusk and the scent carries far. It is grown for its fragrance; I found no Philippine medicinal use recorded for it, and all parts are toxic, so it is not a plant for teas.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
