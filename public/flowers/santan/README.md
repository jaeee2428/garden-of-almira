# Santan — *Ixora coccinea*

jungle geranium, West Indian jasmine · Rubiaceae (coffee family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | faint |
| Bayong order | 4 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#e5381a` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Dense shrub; stiff glossy opposite leaves; round heads of many small 4-petalled star flowers on slender tubes (red, orange, pink, yellow).

**Card text:** Tiny four-petalled flowers in round clusters, a classic hedge in Philippine yards. In folk medicine the root decoction is taken for nausea and hiccups and gargled for sore throat, the flower decoction for dysentery, and fresh leaves are poulticed on sprains, boils and bruises.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
