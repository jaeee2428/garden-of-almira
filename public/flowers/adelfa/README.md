# Adelfa — *Nerium oleander*

oleander · also: baladre · Apocynaceae (dogbane family)

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | light, sweet |
| Bayong order | 15 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#f07aa6` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Shrub 1.5-2 m; narrow leathery leaves in whorls of three; clusters of pinwheel 5-petalled pink or white flowers with a fringed crown at the branch tips. Poisonous if eaten.

**Card text:** Also called baladre: a shrub with narrow leaves in whorls of three and clusters of pink, white or red flowers at the branch tips. Philippine folk medicine uses it only on the skin: crushed leaves in oil on shingles and herpes sores, the juice of young leaves in coconut oil on ringworm, a poultice of pounded leaves on snake bites. Every part is poisonous if swallowed, even to animals, so it is never made into a tea.

Sources: stuartxchange.org/Adelfa.html
