# Liryo sa Tubig — *water lily & lotus*

water lily and sacred lotus · also: liryo sa tubig · Nymphaeaceae & Nelumbonaceae

| | |
|---|---|
| Scent | light |
| Bayong order | 14 of 18 |
| Colour on the card | `#f070a8` |

**What it looks like (the model follows this):** Round floating pads with a slit; star-shaped water-lily flowers on the surface, lotus flowers held above the water on tall stalks.

**Card text:** Water lilies float round pads on still ponds and open their flowers in the morning sun. Calm water draws frogs and dragonflies.

Sources: StuartXchange (Philippine medicinal plants), PTKDL
