# Hardin ni Almira · Thesis proposal

- `index.html` the proposal deck (22 slides). Open in Chrome, press F for full screen.
- `game-flow.html` the game flow plan with clickable mockups, chapter map, poem plan and the research behind each design choice.

Deck keys: → or Space next, ← back, 1 to 9 jump, N speaker notes, F full screen.
Move the mouse over the garden to part the grass and scatter petals; at night the fireflies follow it. Click empty space for a petal burst.
Slide 10 has a "Play example" button. Slide 18 can open the live garden (local server first, then the deployed site).

PDF: Cmd+P, Save as PDF, turn on Background graphics. One slide per page.
